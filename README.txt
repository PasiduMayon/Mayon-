MAYON PORTFOLIO
===============

OPEN IT
  Double-click  index.html  (it works by itself – everything is inside that one file).

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html                         The complete website (code + photo + logos + CV inside)
  Pasidu_Mayon_Batukumara_CV.pdf     A copy of your CV
  images/                            Copies of your photo, logo and tab icon (for your records)

EDIT IT
  Open index.html in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code...) and use Ctrl+F to search:
    "YOUR INFORMATION"  -> names, jobs, links, social accounts, photos
    "COLORS & FONTS"    -> website colours
    "ASSETS"            -> (bottom of file) your photo, logo and CV

PUBLISH IT
  Upload index.html to any free host (Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages)
  and connect your domain www.mayonb.site.

NOTE
  Your project photos load from your old Wix links, so they need internet.
  Download them and replace the links in "YOUR INFORMATION" if you close the Wix site.
